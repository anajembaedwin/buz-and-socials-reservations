Your quote

What you get after you send a request.

Every quote shows

  • The full price for your dates, with what is included and what is not.
  • The meals, the room, hall, fare or vehicle you asked for, and any deal that applies.
  • The last day the price is held.
  • The changes and cancellation rules for that rate.

We send your quote to your WhatsApp or email with a booking number. To accept it, reply yes and pay by bank transfer. To change something, tell us and we send a new quote.
