Questions

Before you book

Why are there no prices on the site?

Hotel, hall and airfare rates change by the day. We check today's rate for your dates and send you the full price, so you never pay a number that is out of date.

When do I pay?

After you receive a quote and say yes. There is no payment when you send a request.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to the account on your quote. Use your booking number as the reference.

Can I change or cancel?

It depends on the rate you book. Your quote lists the changes and cancellation terms before you say yes.

Do you book for groups?

Yes. Teams, families and wedding parties can book several rooms, halls or vehicles on one quote.
