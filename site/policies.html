Changes, cancellations, privacy and terms

Plain words, so you know where you stand before you pay.

Changes and cancellations

Hotel changes and cancellations

Flexible rates can be changed or cancelled free until 48 hours before check-in. Non-refundable rates cannot be refunded. Your quote says which rate you are getting.

Event centres

A deposit holds the date, and the balance is due before the event. Your quote states the deposit and when the balance is due. Where a deposit is not refundable, we will try to move it to a new date.

Shortlets

Changes follow the terms on your quote. A refundable caution deposit may be paid on arrival and returned after check-out if there is no damage.

Airport pickups

Free changes until 12 hours before pickup. Waiting beyond 60 minutes after landing is charged per hour. If your flight is delayed, we move the pickup at no cost.

Flights

Changes and refunds follow the fare rules of the airline. Each fare on our flights page shows what is allowed. We pass on the airline's own charges.

Payment and terms

Payment

You pay by bank transfer to the account on your quote, using your booking number as the reference. We confirm once the money reaches us.

Quotes expire

A quote is held for a limited time, shown on the quote. After that the rate may change.

Prices

Prices are confirmed on your quote. We will tell you before accepting any payment if something about the price changes.

Who we are

Buz and Socials Reservations books hotels, shortlets, event centres, flights and pickups for you. The hotel, event centre or airline provides the service itself.

Privacy

What we collect

Your name, WhatsApp number or email, and the details of the booking you ask for. We collect nothing else.

How we use it

Only to answer your request and to book on your behalf. We share it with the hotel, event centre or airline you choose, and with no one else.

Your choice

Ask us to delete your details at any time and we will.
